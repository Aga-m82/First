Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Aga's Digital Work Studio, run by Aga, a sole trader based in Co. Dublin, Ireland. If you have a question about your data, message me through the chat on this website.

What I collect and why

  • When you buy: your name, email address and payment details are collected by Stripe or PayPal. I receive your name, email, what you bought and the amount, so I can send your product and keep my business records (I have to keep sales records for tax).
  • When you use the chat: your messages are sent to an AI service (Claude, run by Anthropic) so it can answer you. Please don't type personal or payment details into the chat.
  • When you leave a message for me through the chat: your name, email and message are stored by my website host (Netlify) and emailed to me, so I can reply.
  • When you visit the site: my host keeps basic technical logs (like IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google.

I don't use advertising cookies or tracking. The website remembers small things in your browser only (for example, that you've already seen the chat greeting).

Who I share it with

Only the services that make the shop work: Stripe and PayPal (payments), Netlify (website hosting and contact messages), Anthropic (chat answers) and my email provider. Stripe and PayPal also have their own privacy policies. I never sell your data.

How long I keep it

Order records are kept for 6 years, as Irish tax rules require. Chat contact messages are deleted when they're no longer needed, and at the latest after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how I use it. Message me and I'll reply within a month. If you're not happy with my answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie).

The AI Automation Platform

Everything you type into the platform you buy (prompts, progress, notes) is saved only in your own browser on your own device. I can't see it.
