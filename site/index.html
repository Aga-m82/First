About me

Hi, I’m Aga. I turn data into decisions.

I’ve spent 16 years working in operations at a large international airport, where I now lead daily service delivery for a big team and manage business projects from the first plan to final delivery, keeping them on time, on budget and focused on results. Every shift I plan around the numbers: demand, staffing and risks. That’s how I learned how much good data and smart planning save in time and money. Now I bring that experience to small businesses. I advise owners on how to automate repetitive work, plan and manage their projects, stop quietly losing money, and turn what they know into digital products that keep earning.
