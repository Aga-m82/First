About me

Hi, I’m Aga — I run operations on the numbers.

For 16 years I’ve worked in operations at Dublin Airport, growing from the Facilities team to my current step-up role as Service Delivery Manager, where I lead terminal and landside operations across the campus. Every day I plan around the data — passenger demand, staffing levels and emerging risks — and rebalance resources as the day unfolds. I’ve seen first-hand how good data, clear KPIs and smart planning protect service, time and money.

To go deeper, I completed the Diploma in Data Analytics in Business (Level 7) and the Higher Diploma in Data Analytics (Level 8) at CCT College Dublin, and I’m now studying at master’s level — a Level 9 Postgraduate in Computer Science: AI & Machine Learning, covering machine learning, deep learning, coding and model deployment.

Now I bring that same approach to small businesses: I advise owners on how to automate repetitive work, spot where they’re quietly losing money, and turn what they know into digital products that keep earning.
